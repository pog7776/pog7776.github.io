> VR Electrocution Experience Research Paper

2020

Research paper exploring the behavioral impact of real-world physical consequences in Virtual Reality.


Abstract

This paper explores the impact that high consequence feedback has on a player in VR, and the behavioural changes of the player while playing a game with and without the feedback. Virtual Reality being a highly immersive system, it is an appropriate format to test this kind of feedback and its effects.

Download Paper Source Code

Built in Unity 3D for Windows Mixed Reality with a custom microcontroller circuit controlling hardware shock feedback.
